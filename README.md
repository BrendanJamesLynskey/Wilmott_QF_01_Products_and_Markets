# Products and Markets

Deck 01 of the [Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance &mdash; Companion Series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Wilmott_QF_Hub).

**Live presentation:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Wilmott_QF_01_Products_and_Markets/

A guided tour of chapter 1 of *Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance*
(2nd edition, Wiley, 2007) &mdash; the universe of underlying assets on which
every derivative in the rest of the book is written.

## What's inside

- Equities, dividends, stock splits
- Commodities, currencies (FX), and indices
- Continuous compounding and the discount factor
- Fixed-income basics: zero-coupon bonds, present value
- Forward and futures contracts
- A first taste of no-arbitrage &mdash; deriving $F = S_0 e^{(r-q)T}$
- The forward formula in five different costumes (equities, indices, FX, storable and consumption commodities)
- **Interactive forward-pricing calculator** &mdash; drag the spot, rate, yield and maturity and watch the fair forward, the market mispricing, and the locked-in arbitrage profit update live

Companion to chapter 1 of:
